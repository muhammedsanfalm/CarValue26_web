COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some quick answers about CarValue26.

01

How does car price prediction work?

CarValue26 uses vehicle characteristics such as brand, model, age, mileage, engine, and other specifications to estimate a car's value.

02

Can I compare two used cars?

Yes. The Compare Cars feature allows you to enter details for two vehicles and compare their characteristics.

03

Is the predicted price exact?

No. A predicted price is an estimate based on available data and should be used as a reference, not as a guaranteed selling price.

04

Can I view my previous predictions?

Yes. Signed-in users can access their prediction history and review previous car valuations.
