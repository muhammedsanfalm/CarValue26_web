ABOUT CARVALUE26

Smarter Used-Car Valuation Through Data

Understand your car's estimated value with a data-driven machine learning platform.

CarValue26 is a data-driven used-car price prediction and valuation platform designed to help users understand the estimated market value of pre-owned vehicles. Buying or selling a used car can be challenging because the value of a vehicle depends on many different factors. Brand, model, vehicle age, kilometers driven, fuel type, transmission, mileage, engine capacity, maximum power, seats, and seller type can all influence the price of a vehicle.

CarValue26 brings these factors together in one simple and user-friendly platform. Users can enter the details of a vehicle and receive an estimated price based on patterns learned from historical used-car data. The purpose of the platform is to make car valuation easier to understand while demonstrating how data science and machine learning can be applied to a real-world problem.

The machine-learning component of CarValue26 uses a trained Random Forest Regression approach. Before training, the vehicle dataset is cleaned and prepared so that the information can be used effectively. The system considers numerical and categorical vehicle characteristics to learn relationships between car features and historical selling prices.

CarValue26 is more than a price prediction tool. The platform also provides features such as Compare Cars, Market Insights, Prediction History, and the CarValue AI Assistant. These features are designed to give users a broader understanding of used vehicles and the factors that influence their estimated value.
